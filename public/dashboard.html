One table, three levels: continent, then country, then the states inside it. Requests sit on the country row only — they come from the hourly rollup, which stores a country and not a region, so there is no honest way to split them further. There is no city column and there will not be one: city plus a quiet day starts to identify someone. States stop at the 40 largest, and whatever a country has beyond them is on its own row rather than left to go missing. Continents open on a continent's countries, countries open on their states.
